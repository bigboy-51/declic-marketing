# Copywriting Skill

## Purpose
Create persuasive marketing copy that converts readers into customers or engaged prospects.

## The Framework: BFPS

Before writing ANY copy, stop and answer these questions:

**B - Buyer**: Who reads this?
- Job title, pain points, goals
- What transformation do they want?
- What's their objection?

**F - Framework**: What's the structure?
- Hook (first 3 seconds grab attention)
- Problem (acknowledge the pain)
- Solution (how Declic solves it)
- Proof (social proof, results, testimonials)
- Call-to-Action (clear next step)

**P - Persuasion**: What's the emotional core?
- Fear? (What goes wrong without Declic?)
- Hope? (What becomes possible?)
- Greed? (What do they gain?)
- Status? (Who else uses this?)

**S - Specificity**: Numbers, not adjectives
- "Save 10 hours/week" not "save time"
- "62% increase in portfolio returns" not "improve returns"
- "Used by 15,000+ investors" not "trusted by many"

---

## Before You Write

**Challenge yourself:**
- [ ] Is this the clearest path to the sale?
- [ ] Did I research the buyer or guess?
- [ ] Will they click/open/read the next thing?
- [ ] Does every sentence earn its place?
- [ ] Can I cut 20% and keep the power?

## Copy Types & Formulas

### Sales Page Headline
```
[Transformation/Outcome] + [Specificity] + [Authority]

❌ "Declic Financier - Your Financial Platform"
✅ "Make 50K in passive income this year - Financial professionals trust Declic"
```

### Email Subject Line
```
Max 50 characters. Formula: [Curiosity] or [Benefit] or [Urgency]

❌ "New Feature Release"
✅ "Investors reveal: their $50K portfolio mistake"
✅ "You're missing 62% in returns (here's why)"
```

### Social Media Post
```
Hook (first line) → Problem → Solution → CTA

❌ "Check out our new features!"
✅ "Most people manage their portfolio like it's 1995.
Manual tracking. Spreadsheets. Late nights.
Declic automates it. Takes 10 minutes/week.
[Link] See how →"
```

### Ad Copy (Google/Facebook)
```
Headline: Problem or Outcome
Body: Solution + Proof
CTA: One clear action

Headline: "Double your investment returns (no experience needed)"
Body: "Declic's AI matches your risk profile. 1000+ investors made 50K+ last year."
CTA: "Start Free Trial →"
```

## Quality Checklist

**Before submitting copy:**
- [ ] **Clarity test**: Read aloud. Any sentence confusing? Cut it.
- [ ] **Specificity test**: Replace all adjectives with numbers. ("Soon" → "In 48 hours")
- [ ] **Urgency test**: Does the reader know what to do next? One clear action?
- [ ] **Proof test**: Claims are backed by data, testimonials, or logic
- [ ] **Voice test**: Sounds like Declic (professional, approachable, helpful)
- [ ] **Length test**: Can I cut 25% and keep the punch?
- [ ] **Conversion test**: Every sentence moves toward the goal

## Common Mistakes

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Fix |
|-----------|--------|
| "We're the best" | "62% faster than competitors" |
| "Save time" | "Save 10 hours/week" |
| "Trusted by thousands" | "Used by 12,400+ active investors" |
| "Easy to use" | "First portfolio built in 4 minutes" |
| "Revolutionary AI" | "AI that predicts market dips 48 hours early" |
| Generic headline | Specific outcome + proof |

---

## Output

When asked for copy, provide:

1. **The copy itself** (ready to use)
2. **Buyer context** (who this is for, their pain point)
3. **Why it works** (the persuasion angle)
4. **A/B options** (if testing is needed)
5. **Next step** (what to measure)

---

## Success Metrics

Your copy works if:
- Email: Open rate > 30%, CTR > 3%
- Landing page: Conversion rate > 2%
- Social: Engagement rate > 1%, CTR > 0.5%
- Ad copy: Cost per click < target, quality score 7+

All based on YOUR audience, not industry averages.
