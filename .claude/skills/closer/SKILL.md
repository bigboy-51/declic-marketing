# Closer Skill

## Purpose
Convert leads into customers through strategic email sequences, proven call scripts, and expert objection handling. Close deals faster with psychology-driven techniques.

**2026 Reality:**
- 74% of buyer teams experience internal conflict that stalls deals
- 61% prefer self-service for routine questions (set expectations, reduce back-and-forth)
- AI-enabled sellers are 3.7x more likely to hit quota
- Top performers pause 3+ seconds before responding to objections (thoughtful > reactive)

## The Closer's Psychology Framework

### 5 Psychological Frameworks (Choose Based on Objection Type)

#### 1. Cognitive Reframing
**When to use:** Prospect sees it differently than you

**Technique:** Shift their perspective by recontextualizing the problem

```
Objection: "Your solution is expensive"
❌ Wrong: "No it's not, it's actually affordable"
✅ Right: "I get that. Most busy professionals spend 20+ hours/month managing 
portfolios manually. That's $5K in lost time annually. 
Our fee is $2K/year = $3K net savings + peace of mind."

Frame shift: Cost → Investment that saves money
```

#### 2. Tactical Empathy
**When to use:** They feel unheard or misunderstood

**Technique:** Show deep understanding before selling

```
Objection: "I need to think about it"
❌ Wrong: "What's there to think about? Let's close this."
✅ Right: "That makes total sense. You're probably wondering:
- Will this actually work for my situation?
- Is the risk worth the return?
- What if I change my mind?
These are the RIGHT questions to ask. 
Here's what I'm seeing [specific insight]..."

Frame shift: Brush-off → Legitimate concern (address it)
```

#### 3. Solution Bridging
**When to use:** They want it but see a gap between problem and solution

**Technique:** Connect their specific pain to your specific solution

```
Objection: "I don't think this will work for me"
❌ Wrong: "It will work, trust me"
✅ Right: "Help me understand. You mentioned managing your portfolio 
takes 15+ hours/month (their pain).
Our platform cuts that to 30 minutes/month through automated rebalancing.
For your $200K portfolio, that frees up 14+ hours/month = $5K+ in time back.
How does that land?"

Frame shift: Vague doubt → Specific gap (bridge it with data)
```

#### 4. Social Proof
**When to use:** They're risk-averse or unsure

**Technique:** Reduce risk by showing others like them succeeded

```
Objection: "Has this worked for people like me?"
✅ Right: "Absolutely. Similar to you:
- Sarah, busy executive, $250K portfolio → $45K passive income in 2 years
- James, startup founder, zero investing experience → $50K returns first year
- Lisa, single parent, 10 hours/month to spend → Now 2 hours/month, better results
Their success is actually documented in our case study."

Frame shift: Unknown risk → Proven track record (social proof)
```

#### 5. Curiosity & Value Discovery
**When to use:** You suspect the real objection is hidden

**Technique:** Ask questions to uncover what they REALLY want

```
Objection: "I want to wait until next quarter"
❌ Wrong: "No, let's start now"
✅ Right: "I'm curious—what happens in the next quarter that changes?
Is it:
- Budget approval?
- You want to see more results?
- Uncertainty about the market?
- Or something else?
Help me understand what would make you ready."

Frame shift: Stated objection → Real objection (address the real one)
```

### 2. Call Scripts (Discovery + Closing)

**Philosophy:** Listen 70%, talk 30%. Let them talk themselves into it.

#### Discovery Call Script (First Conversation)

```
[0:00] OPENING (Build rapport, set agenda)
────────────────────────────────────────
"Hi [Name]! Thanks so much for jumping on. 
I know you're busy, so I'll keep this focused. 
We'll spend 15 minutes getting to know your situation, 
and I'll tell you if Declic is a fit. Sound good?"

[Wait for yes]

[1:00] DISCOVERY (Listen for pain + motivation)
────────────────────────────────────────
"Perfect. So, let me ask—what's been your biggest 
frustration with managing your portfolio?"

[LISTEN. Take notes. Don't interrupt.]

Follow-up questions (based on their answer):
- "How long has that been frustrating?"
- "What have you tried so far?"
- "What would 'solved' look like for you?"
- "If we could fix that, what would change?"

Goal: Get them talking about THEIR problem, THEIR goals.
Not your solution yet.

[5:00] BUILD CREDIBILITY (Proof you can help)
────────────────────────────────────────
"I hear you. We see this with [similar profile].
Here's what we found works: [Your insight + proof]
[Specific example: James was frustrated with X, now he...]"

Don't oversell. Just show you've solved this before.

[7:00] CURIOSITY (Make them want what you have)
────────────────────────────────────────
"Based on what you shared, I'm seeing [specific insight].
Here's what most people don't realize: [counterintuitive truth].
That means for someone in your situation, [specific opportunity]."

[Pause. Let that sink in.]

"Does that resonate?"

[Wait for their response. Listen.]

[10:00] BRIDGE (Connect their pain to solution)
────────────────────────────────────────
"So if we could help you [their stated goal], 
and get you [specific benefit], 
would that be worth exploring?"

[Listen for commitment level. Don't push.]

[12:00] CLOSE (Ask for next step, not sale)
────────────────────────────────────────
"Here's what I'd suggest: Let's run a quick assessment.
Takes 10 minutes, and you'll see exactly 
how much passive income you could unlock.

Then we'll hop on a quick call (20 min) to review.

How does that sound?"

[If yes, schedule. If hesitation, use objection handling framework.]

[14:00] CONFIRM (Recap & commitment)
────────────────────────────────────────
"Great. So you'll take the assessment today, 
and we'll chat on [Day/Time] to review. 
I'll send you the link in an email right after this.
Sound good?"

Confirm all details clearly.

Key principles:
✅ Listen more than you talk
✅ Let them tell you why they need it
✅ Ask permission before next step (don't assume)
✅ One clear ask per call
✅ End with scheduled follow-up (not "I'll reach out")
```

### 3. Objection Handling (The Formula)

**The Pattern:**
1. **Pause** (3+ seconds, think before responding)
2. **Empathize** ("I understand why you'd feel that way")
3. **Clarify** ("Help me understand what you mean...")
4. **Reframe** (Use one of the 5 frameworks)
5. **Move Forward** ("So given that, does this make sense?")

#### Common Objections + Responses

**Objection 1: "It's too expensive"**

```
Pause (3 seconds) ✓
Empathize: "I get it. Price matters."
Clarify: "When you say expensive, is it:
  - Compared to other tools?
  - Compared to doing it yourself?
  - Concerns about ROI?
  What's the real concern?"

[Listen to their answer. They'll tell you the real issue.]

Reframe (Solution Bridging):
"Okay, so cost is the concern. Here's what I'd point out:
Your portfolio is [X] size. You're earning [Y]% returns.
With our strategy, similar profiles earn [Z]%.
That's $[difference] more per year.
Our fee is $[fee].
Net gain: $[net]."

Move Forward:
"Given that math, does the investment make sense?"
```

**Objection 2: "I want to think about it"**

```
Pause (3 seconds) ✓
Empathize: "That's smart. This is a decision."
Clarify: "Help me understand—what do you need 
to think about? Is it:
  - Uncertainty about returns?
  - Wondering if it's right for your risk profile?
  - Timing (when to start)?
  - Or something else?"

[Listen.]

Reframe (Cognitive Reframing or Curiosity):
"I'm guessing you're wondering, 'Will this actually work 
for me?' That's exactly the right question.
What if I showed you [specific proof for their situation]?
Would that help you decide?"

[If yes, provide proof]

Move Forward:
"So if you can see it working for someone like you, 
are you ready to move forward?"

[If still hesitant: "What's one more thing that would 
help you feel confident?"]
```

**Objection 3: "I don't have time to manage another thing"**

```
Pause (3 seconds) ✓
Empathize: "I hear you. Busy schedule."
Clarify: "When you say you don't have time, 
are you thinking about:
  - Initial setup (takes 10 min)?
  - Ongoing management (should be < 30 min/month)?
  - Learning curve?"

[Listen.]

Reframe (Solution Bridging):
"I understand. Most people assume it's complicated.
Here's the reality: 
- Setup: 10 minutes (we walk you through it)
- Monthly: 2-3 minutes to approve rebalancing
- That's it. Automation does the rest.

Compared to managing it yourself (15-20 hours/month),
you actually SAVE 14+ hours/month.
That's worth $2K+/month in your time."

Move Forward:
"So if we're actually saving you time, not taking it,
does that change how you see it?"
```

**Objection 4: "I'll do it when [timing] happens"**

```
Pause (3 seconds) ✓
Empathize: "Makes sense you'd wait."
Clarify: "When you say next quarter, is that because:
  - Budget approval is pending?
  - You want to see how the market moves?
  - Testing our track record first?
  - Something else?"

[Listen. The answer tells you the real objection.]

Reframe (Use appropriate framework for their real concern):
If "budget": "Actually, starting now means you hit 
your ROI target BY next quarter, not waiting until then."

If "market": "Timing the market is exactly what costs 
people $50K. Dollar-cost averaging from today is smarter."

If "proof": "Let's prove it with a 30-day trial. 
See the results, then decide."

Move Forward:
"So if we can [address their real concern], 
is there a reason not to start this week?"
```

## Behavioral Economics Triggers (Ethical Application)

### 6 Proven Triggers (Use Responsibly)

```
1. LOSS AVERSION
   Humans fear losses 2x more than gains
   
   ✅ Use: "Waiting costs you $50K in returns"
   ❌ Don't: Fabricate urgency

2. SCARCITY
   Limited availability feels more valuable
   
   ✅ Use: "Onboarding slots limited this month"
   ❌ Don't: Invent false scarcity

3. SOCIAL PROOF
   Others' success influences our decisions
   
   ✅ Use: Real case studies + testimonials
   ❌ Don't: Fake reviews

4. RECIPROCITY
   We want to return favors
   
   ✅ Use: Give free assessment, they feel obligated
   ❌ Don't: Manipulate through fake freebies

5. ANCHORING
   First number influences perception
   
   ✅ Use: "Alternative costs $5K, ours is $2K"
   ❌ Don't: Misleading price comparisons

6. FRAMING
   How information is presented changes perception
   
   ✅ Use: "Save $50K/year" vs. "$2K investment"
   ❌ Don't: Hide important information
```

## Quality Checklist

**Before using any close:**
- [ ] **Genuine**: Not manipulative, actually helps them
- [ ] **Specific**: Uses their situation, not generic script
- [ ] **Empathetic**: Acknowledges their concern first
- [ ] **Data-backed**: Real numbers, not vague promises
- [ ] **One ask**: Only one clear next step per conversation
- [ ] **Patient**: Pauses, listens, doesn't rush
- [ ] **Followable**: Can be repeated consistently

## Success Metrics

**Your closing is working if:**
- **Call-to-close rate**: > 30% (1 in 3 calls converts)
- **Email-to-close**: > 5% (reasonable benchmark)
- **Objection handling**: > 60% of objections resolved (not all, just most)
- **Sales cycle length**: 7-14 days (for SaaS)

**A/B Testing:**
- Test email subject: Generic vs. Specific
- Test call opening: Hard sell vs. Discovery-first
- Test objection response: Framework A vs. Framework B
- Test close timing: Call on Day 3 vs. Day 5
