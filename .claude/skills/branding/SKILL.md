# Branding Skill

## Purpose
Create and maintain a cohesive brand identity that customers immediately recognize and trust.

## Before Branding

**Challenge:**
- [ ] What problem does Declic solve that competitors don't?
- [ ] Who is the customer? (Not "everyone")
- [ ] What emotion should they feel when they see our brand?
- [ ] What should they remember after 6 months of not seeing us?

If you can't answer these clearly, branding is premature.

---

## The Brand Foundation

### Positioning Statement
```
[Target] who want [outcome] should choose Declic because [unique reason]

❌ "For investors who want better returns"
✅ "For busy professionals who want 50K+ annual passive income without daily monitoring, because Declic's AI does the research and rebalancing automatically"
```

### Brand Promise
What do customers get every single time they interact with Declic?

```
Clarity: Our platform explains every decision, no black boxes
Control: You decide the risk level, not us
Results: Your money works harder than traditional approaches
```

## Visual Identity System

### Colors (5 max, 2 primary)

**Why it matters**: Colors trigger emotion before words are read.

```
Primary: #2563EB (Trust, technology, professional)
Secondary: #10B981 (Growth, money, success)
Accent: #F59E0B (Action, attention, warmth)
Neutral: #1F2937 (Text), #F3F4F6 (Background)
```

**Usage:**
- Primary: Headers, CTAs, key UI
- Secondary: Growth metrics, success states
- Accent: Alerts, important info
- Neutral: Everything else

### Typography (2 families max)

```
Headlines: Inter (geometric, modern, clean)
  Weights: Bold (700), SemiBold (600)
  Sizes: H1: 48px, H2: 32px, H3: 24px

Body: Inter (same font, maintain consistency)
  Weight: Regular (400)
  Size: 16px
  Line height: 1.6 (readability)
```

### Photography/Imagery

```
Style: Real people, real situations (no stock photos)
Tone: Professional but human, approachable, smart
Ratio: 16:9 landscape preferred
Color grading: Warm, slightly desaturated
```

## Tone of Voice

### Brand Personality (3 words)

```
Expert, Approachable, Direct
```

### In Practice

| Scenario | Voice | Example |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Error message | Helpful, clear | "Your portfolio needs 2 more stocks for diversification. Here's why." Not "Error 402" |
| Celebrating wins | Genuine, warm | "Your portfolio beat the S&P by 12% this quarter. Nice." Not "Congratulations on your success!" |
| Explaining complex topic | Expert but simple | "Volatility = how much your portfolio bounces. Higher = bigger swings = higher potential returns." |

### Do's and Don'ts

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|------|---------|
| Use "you" and "we" | Use "customer" or "user" |
| Be specific | Use corporate jargon |
| Show personality | Be overly casual or hip |
| Explain the why | Assume the reader understands |
| Use contractions | Sound robotic |
| Use metaphors | Use made-up words |

## Brand Applications

### Website
- Logo: Top-left, clear
- Colors: Primary on CTAs, secondary on metrics
- Typography: Consistent hierarchy
- Tone: Expert but accessible
- Voice: Direct, benefit-focused

### Email
- Subject: Direct benefit ("62% better returns in 6 months")
- Signature: Simple, branded
- Tone: One-on-one, helpful
- CTA: Clear, consistent button style

### Social Media
- Instagram: Visual-first, behind-the-scenes, market insights
- LinkedIn: Thought leadership, data-driven posts, CEO perspective
- Twitter: Real-time market reactions, quick tips, personality
- TikTok: Educational, lightning-fast, personality-forward

### Advertising
- Headline: Specific benefit
- Visuals: Real people, real results
- CTA: One clear action
- Tone: Confident, proven

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Positioning statement is unique and defensible
- [ ] Brand promise is kept every interaction
- [ ] Color palette works in B&W (accessibility)
- [ ] Typography scales from mobile to desktop
- [ ] Imagery is consistent in quality and tone
- [ ] Voice guidelines are clear enough that a new hire could follow them
- [ ] All touchpoints reinforce the same brand

---

## Common Mistakes

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Fix |
|-----------|--------|
| Logo in every piece | Logo in header, smaller elsewhere |
| Too many colors | 5 colors maximum |
| Stock photos | Real people and real situations |
| Generic tone | Specific to Declic's personality |
| Inconsistent guidelines | One source of truth (this doc) |
| Vague positioning | "For X who want Y because Z" |

---

## Success Metrics

Your brand is working if:
- [ ] Customers recognize Declic in a crowded feed
- [ ] New hires understand brand voice after 1 hour
- [ ] All marketing materials look like they belong together
- [ ] Net Promoter Score increases (brand loyalty)
- [ ] Ad recall improves (people remember the brand, not just the offer)

---

## Output

When defining or updating branding, provide:

1. **Positioning statement** (one sentence, defensible)
2. **Brand promise** (what we deliver every time)
3. **Visual system** (colors, fonts, imagery rules)
4. **Voice guidelines** (dos and don'ts with examples)
5. **Application templates** (how to use on each channel)
6. **Quick reference** (one-page guide for teams)
