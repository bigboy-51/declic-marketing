# Trading Pro Skill

## Purpose

Trade with **expert-level discipline and analysis**.

This skill is for traders who:
- Read charts fluently (support/resistance, trends, patterns)
- Understand macro (Fed policy, earnings, economic cycles)
- Manage risk religiously (position sizing, stops, diversification)
- Control emotions (psychology > ego)
- Think in probabilities (not certainties)

**2026 Reality:**
- 90% of day traders lose money
- Top 10% make >$100K/year consistently
- Difference: Risk management + discipline + macro awareness
- Best traders spend 80% on analysis, 20% on execution
- Psychology determines 70% of success (discipline beats genius)

## The Trading Framework: 3 Layers

```
LAYER 1: MARKET STRUCTURE (Macro)
├─ Economic cycles (expansion, peak, contraction, trough)
├─ Fed policy (interest rates, inflation, policy shifts)
├─ Sector rotation (what's in favor right now?)
├─ Macro trends (inflation, employment, geopolitical)
└─ Central bank actions (most powerful force)

LAYER 2: TECHNICAL ANALYSIS (Chart Reading)
├─ Trend identification (up, down, sideways)
├─ Support/resistance (where price bounces/breaks)
├─ Chart patterns (head & shoulders, double bottom, flags)
├─ Volume analysis (is this move backed by volume?)
├─ Momentum indicators (is trend gaining/losing strength?)

LAYER 3: RISK MANAGEMENT (Execute Safely)
├─ Position sizing (how much to risk per trade)
├─ Stop losses (where to exit if wrong)
├─ Risk-reward ratio (minimum 1:2, best 1:3)
├─ Portfolio allocation (diversification across assets)
└─ Emotional discipline (stick to rules, no revenge trading)
```

## Layer 1: Macro Analysis (Economic Context)

### Understanding Economic Cycles

**The 4-Phase Cycle:**

```
PHASE 1: EXPANSION (Economic growth accelerating)
├─ GDP growing >2%/year
├─ Unemployment falling
├─ Corporate profits rising
├─ Fed NOT raising rates (or early in hiking cycle)
├─ Market direction: UP 📈
├─ Best sectors: Growth stocks (tech), cyclicals
└─ Your action: BUY strong stocks, hold

PHASE 2: PEAK (Economy overheating)
├─ GDP growth >3%/year
├─ Unemployment very low (<4%)
├─ Inflation appearing
├─ Fed raising rates aggressively
├─ Market direction: CHOPPY, volatile
├─ Warning signs: VIX spiking, yield curve flattening
└─ Your action: Reduce leverage, tighten stops, watch bonds

PHASE 3: CONTRACTION (Growth slowing)
├─ GDP slowing (growth <2%)
├─ Unemployment rising
├─ Corporate profits contracting
├─ Fed pausing/cutting rates
├─ Market direction: DOWN 📉
├─ First to fall: Cyclicals, then growth
└─ Your action: SHORT weak stocks, increase cash, avoid leverage

PHASE 4: TROUGH (Recession bottom)
├─ GDP negative
├─ Unemployment spike
├─ Profits in recovery/stabilization
├─ Fed cutting rates aggressively
├─ Market direction: BOTTOMING (most volatile)
├─ Opportunity: Massive reversals (short squeeze)
└─ Your action: Watch for reversal signals, small short-covering buys
```

### Key Macro Indicators to Watch

**Most Important (Check Weekly):**

```
1. FED POLICY (Determines everything)
   ├─ Interest rate: Is it rising, stable, or falling?
   ├─ If rising: Bad for growth stocks, good for bonds
   ├─ If falling: Good for growth stocks, bad for bonds
   ├─ Policy stance: Hawkish (tightening) or Dovish (easing)?
   └─ Impact on your trading: MASSIVE
   
2. INFLATION DATA (CPI/PCE)
   ├─ Is it rising or falling?
   ├─ If rising: Fed will raise rates (stocks down)
   ├─ If falling: Fed can cut rates (stocks up)
   ├─ What traders watch: Month-over-month change
   └─ Surprise > 0.5% = Market shock
   
3. EMPLOYMENT DATA (Non-Farm Payroll)
   ├─ How many new jobs created?
   ├─ If >200K: Economy strong, Fed stays tight
   ├─ If <100K: Economy weakening, Fed cuts coming
   ├─ Unemployment rate: Trend matters more than level
   └─ Surprise = Market moves +2-3%
   
4. EARNINGS SEASON (Quarterly)
   ├─ Do stocks meet/beat estimates?
   ├─ What's guidance (forward outlook)?
   ├─ Rising guidance = Buy signal
   ├─ Lowering guidance = Sell signal
   └─ Earnings beats overrated, guidance is truth
   
5. CORPORATE BOND YIELDS (Credit Health)
   ├─ If spreads widening = Companies stressed = Stock weakness
   ├─ If spreads tightening = Companies strong = Stock strength
   └─ Use TLT (20-year bonds) as canary in coal mine
```

**Trading Rule:**
```
If macro is against you, don't fight it.
Example: Fed raising rates + inflation up = 
Market likely down. Don't go long on hopium.

Example: Fed cutting rates + inflation falling = 
Market likely up. Don't stay short.
```

## Layer 2: Technical Analysis (Chart Reading)

### The 5 Most Reliable Patterns

#### **Pattern 1: Support & Resistance**

```
Support: Price level where buyers step in (floor)
Resistance: Price level where sellers step in (ceiling)

Chart example:
Stock bounced off $100 three times ← Strong support
Failed to break $120 three times ← Strong resistance

Trading rules:
✅ Buy near support (lower risk, higher probability)
✅ Sell near resistance (lower risk, higher probability)
❌ Don't buy near resistance (fighting sellers)
❌ Don't sell near support (fighting buyers)

Test: $100 support holds? Stock likely up
      $100 support breaks + volume? Stock likely down 30%+
```

#### **Pattern 2: Trend Identification**

```
UPTREND: Lower lows getting higher, higher highs getting higher
├─ Chart: Looks like stairs going up /̅/̅/̅
├─ Trading: BUY near support on pullbacks
├─ Risk: Buy only if trend is intact
├─ Exit: Trend breaks (lower low below previous low)

DOWNTREND: Higher highs getting lower, higher lows getting lower
├─ Chart: Looks like stairs going down \̅\̅\̅
├─ Trading: SHORT near resistance on bounces
├─ Risk: Short only if trend is intact
├─ Exit: Trend breaks (higher high above previous high)

SIDEWAYS: Price bouncing between support + resistance
├─ Chart: Looks like a box ├─────┤
├─ Trading: Boring (avoid), or buy support/sell resistance
├─ Risk: Breakout can be violent (either direction)
├─ Exit: Break one side with volume (true breakout)
```

#### **Pattern 3: Head & Shoulders (Top Reversal)**

```
      HEAD (High point in middle)
      /\      /\
    /  \___/  \    ← Both shoulders same height
  /___\     ___\

This means: Uptrend is BREAKING, likely reversal to down

Setup:
1. Left shoulder: Price up, then down (pullback)
2. Head: Price up higher, then down to same level as shoulder
3. Right shoulder: Price up, but lower than head, then down

Confirmation:
✅ Volume decreasing on rallies (weak buying)
✅ Volume increasing on sells (strong selling)
✅ Neckline breaks with volume (breakout downward)

Trade:
- Short when neckline breaks (at low point of pattern)
- Stop loss: Above right shoulder high
- Target: Distance from neckline to head = downside potential
```

#### **Pattern 4: Double Bottom (Bottom Reversal)**

```
    _____
   /     \    /     \    ← Both lows same price (support)
  /       \__/       \

This means: Downtrend is BREAKING, likely reversal to up

Setup:
1. First decline to support level ($100)
2. Bounce up (relief rally)
3. Second decline to same support ($100)
4. Bounce up and break above resistance (peak between two lows)

Confirmation:
✅ Volume drying up on second decline (less selling)
✅ Volume exploding on bounce from second bottom (strong buying)
✅ Resistance breaks with volume (new uptrend begins)

Trade:
- Buy after second bottom breaks resistance
- Stop loss: Below both bottoms ($95)
- Target: Distance from bottom to resistance = upside potential
```

#### **Pattern 5: Flag (Continuation)**

```
Flagpole: Sharp move up or down
Flag: Consolidation (pause) in opposite direction

Example (Bullish Flag):
  |
  |  /  ← Flagpole (sharp up)
  | / __
  |/   | ← Flag (consolidation in small range)
       |

Meaning: Uptrend pausing to consolidate before continuing up

Trade:
- Buy breakout above flag (with volume)
- Stop: Below flag support
- Target: Flagpole height (momentum likely to repeat)

Example: Stock up $10 sharp, then consolidates $2 range
         Breaks above consolidation → Likely up another $10
```

### Indicators That Actually Work

**Best Indicators (Proven to Work):**

```
1. MOVING AVERAGES
   50-day MA: Intermediate trend
   200-day MA: Long-term trend
   
   Golden Cross: 50 MA crosses above 200 MA = BUY signal
   Death Cross: 50 MA crosses below 200 MA = SELL signal
   
   Accuracy: ~55-60% win rate (not amazing, but consistent)

2. RSI (Relative Strength Index)
   0-30: Oversold (potential bounce)
   30-70: Normal range
   70-100: Overbought (potential pullback)
   
   Use: Buy oversold (RSI <30), Sell overbought (RSI >70)
   Best: Works in ranging markets, not trends
   Accuracy: ~60-65% win rate

3. VOLUME
   Volume up = Real move (backed by money)
   Volume down = Fake move (not backed by money)
   
   Rule: Don't trust a move unless volume confirms it
   
   Example: Stock up 5% but volume flat = Ignore (likely reverses)
            Stock up 5% + volume 2x normal = Real (likely continues)

4. VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)
   Tells you: Fair price based on volume at each level
   
   Use: Buy near VWAP (fair price), Sell far from VWAP
   Accuracy: High, but only for day/swing trades

5. MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
   Shows: Momentum change
   
   Use: MACD line crosses signal line = momentum change
   Best: Catching trend changes early
   Accuracy: ~55-60% win rate
```

**Indicators That DON'T Work (Avoid):**

```
❌ Bollinger Bands (Too many false signals)
❌ Stochastic (Whipsaws in choppy markets)
❌ Fibonacci (Works sometimes, not predictive)
❌ Anything with >3 inputs (Over-optimized, doesn't work real-time)
```

## Layer 3: Risk Management (The Most Important)

### Position Sizing (How Much to Risk Per Trade)

**The Rule: Never Risk More Than 1-2% of Portfolio Per Trade**

```
Portfolio: $100,000
Risk per trade: 1% = $1,000 maximum loss

Example Trade:
├─ Entry: $50 (Buy 100 shares = $5,000)
├─ Stop loss: $48 (Lose $2 per share)
├─ Risk: 100 shares × $2 = $200 total loss (0.2% of portfolio)
├─ Target: $55 (Make $5 per share)
├─ Reward: 100 shares × $5 = $500 total gain
├─ Risk-reward: $500/$200 = 2.5:1 ✅ (Good)
└─ Expected value: 50% win rate × $500 - 50% × $200 = $150 per trade

After 10 trades:
├─ If 50% win rate: $150 × 10 = $1,500 profit
├─ Portfolio: $100,000 → $101,500
└─ Compounding works if you stick to position sizing
```

### Stop Losses (Essential, Non-Negotiable)

**The Rule: Always Have a Stop Loss**

```
Types of stops:

1. HARD STOP (Most disciplined)
   Set stop before entering
   Automatically exit if hit
   Non-negotiable (no emotion)
   
   Example:
   Entry: $50
   Stop: $48 (2 points away)
   Exit automatically if price touches $48

2. TRAILING STOP (For runners)
   Follows price as it goes up
   Locks in profits
   Exits on pullback of X%
   
   Example:
   Stock enters at $50, runs to $60
   Trailing stop: 5% = $57
   If price drops to $57, exit with $7 gain

3. TIME STOP (For patience)
   Exit if trade hasn't worked in X days
   Prevents "hope trades"
   
   Example:
   Trade setup: Bounce from support
   Time stop: 5 days
   If no move in 5 days, exit (find better setup)
```

### Risk-Reward Ratio (Minimum 1:2)

```
Good trade: Risk $100 to make $200+ (1:2 ratio minimum)
Bad trade: Risk $100 to make $50 (1:0.5 ratio - avoid)

How to structure:

Entry + Stop = Risk amount
Entry + Target = Reward amount

Example:
├─ Entry: $100
├─ Stop: $95 (Risk: $5 per share × 100 = $500)
├─ Target: $110 (Reward: $10 per share × 100 = $1,000)
├─ Risk-reward: $1,000/$500 = 2:1 ✅ (Good)
└─ Win rate needed: 33%+ to be profitable

With 1:2 ratio and 50% win rate:
├─ 10 trades: 5 winners, 5 losers
├─ Wins: 5 × $1,000 = $5,000
├─ Losses: 5 × $500 = $2,500
├─ Net: $2,500 profit (25% return)
```

### Diversification (Don't Put All Eggs in One Basket)

```
Portfolio allocation:

Conservative (Low risk, slow growth):
├─ 60% Broad market (SPY/VOO/QQQ)
├─ 25% Bonds (BND/AGG)
├─ 10% Individual stocks
├─ 5% Speculative (crypto, options)

Moderate (Balanced growth):
├─ 50% Broad market
├─ 15% Bonds
├─ 25% Individual stocks
├─ 10% Speculative

Aggressive (High growth, high risk):
├─ 40% Broad market
├─ 5% Bonds
├─ 35% Individual stocks
├─ 20% Speculative (crypto, options, small caps)

Rule: Never put more than 5-10% in single stock
```

## Timeframes: Day / Swing / Position Trading

### Day Trading (Intraday, exit same day)

**Characteristics:**
```
Duration: Minutes to hours
Risk: High (leverage, slippage, emotions)
Advantage: No overnight gap risk
Disadvantage: Requires constant attention, high costs
```

**Setup:**
```
Strategy: Trade support/resistance bounces

1. Identify key support/resistance (from previous day + intraday)
2. Wait for price to test support/resistance
3. Enter on bounce with volume confirmation
4. Stop: Just below support (if long)
5. Target: Next resistance
6. Exit: Before close (never hold overnight)

Example:
├─ SPY opened $420
├─ Found support at $419
├─ Bounced to $419.50
├─ Entered long at $419.60 with volume
├─ Target: $420.50 (resistance)
├─ Stop: $418.80
├─ Exit at $420.40 (just before resistance)
├─ Profit: $0.80 per share (need 100+ shares to be worth it)

Time: 2 hours max
Risk: 0.5% of portfolio ($500 on $100K)
Reward: Aim for 1-2% per trade ($500-$1,000)
```

**When to Day Trade:**
```
✅ Market trending (clear direction)
✅ Support/resistance obvious
✅ Volume backing moves
✅ You have time to monitor

❌ Don't day trade in:
   - Choppy/sideways market
   - Low volume
   - During earnings
   - If you can't monitor (day job)
```

### Swing Trading (3-5 days, capitalize on trend)

**Characteristics:**
```
Duration: 3-10 days
Risk: Medium (trend-following, hold overnight)
Advantage: Less time-intensive than day trading
Disadvantage: Overnight gap risk
```

**Setup:**
```
Strategy: Ride the trend

1. Identify trend (uptrend or downtrend)
2. Wait for pullback in trend (consolidation)
3. Enter on continuation (break above/below consolidation)
4. Stop: Below pullback low (if long in uptrend)
5. Target: Previous resistance (or next level)
6. Hold 3-10 days

Example:
├─ Stock in uptrend: $50 → $60 → $55 (pullback) → entering now
├─ Support at $53 (low of pullback)
├─ Resistance above $63
├─ Entry: $55.50 (break above pullback high of $55)
├─ Stop: $52.50 (below support)
├─ Target: $63 (previous resistance)
├─ Risk: $3 per share
├─ Reward: $7.50 per share (2.5:1)
├─ Hold: 4 days
├─ Exit: $63 (at resistance)

Result: +$7.50 per share (5-day return)
```

**When to Swing Trade:**
```
✅ Market trending (clear direction)
✅ You can't day trade (have job)
✅ Higher probability than day trading

❌ Avoid when:
   - Earnings coming (gap risk)
   - Major macro news expected
   - Consolidating/choppy
```

### Position Trading (Weeks to months, buy and hold)

**Characteristics:**
```
Duration: 2 weeks to 6 months
Risk: Lower per-trade (trend-following, stop far)
Advantage: Less monitoring, let winners run
Disadvantage: Need to hold through volatility
```

**Setup:**
```
Strategy: Buy breakouts, hold strong trend

1. Identify long-term trend (6-12 month view)
2. Buy breakouts of consolidation (with volume)
3. Stop: Far below (let trade breathe)
4. Target: Major resistance level
5. Hold weeks/months

Example (SPY):
├─ SPY broke above previous high of $425 (with volume)
├─ Entering: $425.50
├─ Stop: $415 (10 points, lets trade have room)
├─ Target: $450 (next major resistance)
├─ Potential: +$24.50 per share
├─ Hold period: 6-12 weeks
├─ Exit: At $450 or when trend breaks

Result: 5.8% return over 2-3 months
Annualized: ~23% (if repeatable)
```

**When to Position Trade:**
```
✅ Long-term trend is up
✅ You can hold through volatility (dips 5-10%)
✅ Macro supports it (Fed easing, earnings growing)

❌ Avoid when:
   - Macro headwinds (Fed tightening, recession risk)
   - You need quick exits (emergency cash)
```

## Trading Psychology (The Secret Weapon)

### The 3 Emotions That Destroy Traders

**Emotion 1: Fear**
```
Symptom: Exiting winners too early
Example: Stock up $100, you close at $50 profit
        Later it goes to $500 without you

Fix:
- Let winners run (use trailing stops)
- Take partial profits (sell 1/3 at first target, hold 2/3)
- Trust your technical setup (if trend intact, stay)
```

**Emotion 2: Greed**
```
Symptom: Oversizing, not taking profits
Example: Won $100, now betting $200 on next trade
        Lose and give back all profits + more

Fix:
- Stick to 1-2% risk rule religiously
- Take profits at target (don't be greedy)
- Never add to losing positions
```

**Emotion 3: Hope**
```
Symptom: Holding losing trades, waiting for bounce
Example: Stock down 10%, "I'll wait for it to come back"
        It goes down another 20%

Fix:
- Use hard stops (exit if hit)
- Don't hold past time stop (if setup fails, exit)
- Close losers and find better setups
```

### Rules to Remember

```
1. THE STOP LOSS IS YOUR BEST FRIEND
   Without it: Losses can be unlimited
   With it: Losses are known, controlled, manageable

2. BIGGER WINS COME FROM DISCIPLINE, NOT LUCK
   Best traders: Boring, consistent, rule-based
   Worst traders: Emotional, random, "gut feelings"

3. CONSISTENCY BEATS ACCURACY
   Bad trader: 70% win rate but loses money (position sizing)
   Good trader: 50% win rate but makes money (risk-reward)
   
   Formula: (Win% × Avg Win) - (Loss% × Avg Loss) = Expected Value
   Focus on this, not win rate

4. THE MARKET DOESN'T OWE YOU ANYTHING
   Some days you'll lose on good setups (bad luck)
   Some days you'll win on bad setups (good luck)
   Over 100+ trades, edge becomes obvious

5. ONE RULE > ALL THE RULES
   If you follow ONLY one rule: Always use position sizing + stops
   This alone prevents catastrophic losses
```

## Quality Checklist

**Before Every Trade:**

- [ ] **Macro check:** Is macro aligned? (Fed policy, inflation, trend)
- [ ] **Trend check:** Is there a clear trend? (up, down, or sideways?)
- [ ] **Support/Resistance:** Is entry near support or above resistance?
- [ ] **Volume:** Is volume backing this move?
- [ ] **Risk-reward:** Is reward at least 2x the risk?
- [ ] **Position size:** Am I risking ≤1-2% portfolio?
- [ ] **Stop loss:** Do I have a hard stop set?
- [ ] **Time:** Do I have time to monitor this trade?
- [ ] **News:** Are earnings or macro news coming?

**Before You Trade, Not After.**
