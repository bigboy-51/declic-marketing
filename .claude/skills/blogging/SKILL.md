# Blogging Skill

## Purpose
Build authority and organic traffic through high-quality, SEO-optimized articles that answer reader questions and solve real problems.

## Before Writing

**Challenge:**
- [ ] What question does this article answer?
- [ ] Who is searching for this (real search volume)?
- [ ] Why should Declic be the authority on this?
- [ ] What action do we want after they read?
- [ ] Can we prove claims with data?

If you can't articulate these, the article won't work.

---

## The Blog Formula (with EEAT + Topical Authority)

### Headline Structure
```
[Benefit/Outcome] + [Specificity] + [Promise]

❌ "Investment Tips"
✅ "How to Make $50K Passive Income: The Data-Driven Portfolio Strategy"
```

### 2026 Ranking Factors: The Trinity

1. **Technical Performance** (Core Web Vitals)
2. **Content Depth** (topical authority, not single keywords)
3. **EEAT** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)

Focus on covering ENTIRE TOPICS in depth, not just ranking for single keywords. Google rewards structured content ecosystems that show topical authority.

### Article Structure (Golden Path)

1. **Hook** (50 words)
   - Why should they keep reading?
   - Problem statement or curiosity gap
   - "By the end of this article, you'll know..."

2. **Credibility** (100 words)
   - Why should they trust you?
   - Data point, personal story, or authority signal
   - "I analyzed 12,000 portfolios and found..."

3. **The Main Content** (800-2000 words)
   - Divide into 3-5 clear sections
   - Each section: one main idea
   - Use headers, bullets, emphasis
   - Include 1 table or chart per 500 words

4. **Proof** (100 words)
   - Data, testimonials, or case studies
   - "Here's what happened when..."
   - Specific numbers only

5. **Call-to-Action** (50 words)
   - One clear next step
   - Why they should take it
   - Make it specific, not vague

## Content Quality

### The Clarity Test
Read paragraph aloud. If you stumble, readers will too. Rewrite.

| ❌ Unclear | ✅ Clear |
|-----------|---------|
| "Optimize your asset allocation strategy" | "Spread your money across 8-12 stocks to reduce risk" |
| "Leverage volatility dynamics" | "When stocks drop, buy more (your average cost drops)" |
| "Rebalancing mechanisms" | "Rebalance quarterly: sell winners, buy losers" |

### The Specificity Test
No vague claims. All data-backed.

| ❌ Vague | ✅ Specific |
|---------|-----------|
| "Most investors underperform" | "78% of active traders underperform the S&P 500 annually" |
| "You can make more money" | "By switching to AI rebalancing, you could earn $12K more per year" |
| "Better results" | "Our data shows 15% higher returns vs. manual portfolios" |

## SEO (Without Cheating)

### Keyword Research
- What are people actually searching for?
- Use Google Trends, Google Search Console, SEMrush
- Target 1 primary keyword + 3-5 secondary keywords
- Include in: headline, first 100 words, headers, conclusion

### On-Page Elements
```
Title: 50-60 characters, primary keyword first
Meta: 150-160 characters, benefit + curiosity
Slug: /how-to-make-50k-passive-income/
Headers: H2s include secondary keywords
Links: 3-5 internal links, 2-3 external (authoritative sources)
Images: Alt text includes keywords
```

### Structure for Scanning (Featured Snippet Optimization)

- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Bolded key phrases
- Bullet points over paragraphs
- Headers every 300 words (include secondary keywords)
- One visual per 500 words
- **Summary sections** (extractable for featured snippets)
- **Clear definitions** (helps AI extract content)

## 2026 SEO Metrics to Track (Not Vanity Metrics)

**Stop tracking:**
- Bounce rate (replaced by engagement time in GA4)

**Start tracking:**
- **Organic traffic** (actual visitors)
- **Average engagement time** (honest attention metric)
- **Conversions** (revenue-tied goal completions)
- **Core Web Vitals** (LCP, INP, CLS - ranking factors)
- **Keyword rankings** (but focus on topics, not keywords)
- **Referring domains** (backlink authority)
- **SERP feature visibility** (featured snippets, knowledge panel)

**Planning approach:** Quarterly planning balances long-term strategy with tactical execution. This prevents reactive scrambling.

## Types of Blog Posts

### How-To Article
```
Headline: "How to [Specific Outcome] in [Timeframe]"
Structure:
  1. Why people need this
  2. What they'll need (tools, time, knowledge)
  3. Step-by-step process (5-8 steps)
  4. Common mistakes to avoid
  5. Next level (advanced tips)
  
Example: "How to Build a $100K Investment Portfolio in 18 Months"
```

### Data-Driven Article
```
Headline: "[Finding] According to [Your] Analysis of [Sample Size]"
Structure:
  1. Thesis statement
  2. Methodology (how you gathered data)
  3. Key findings (biggest insight first)
  4. What it means
  5. What to do about it

Example: "We Analyzed 10,000 Portfolios and Found One Strategy Outperforms 78% of the Time"
```

### Thought Leadership
```
Headline: "Why [Common Belief] is Wrong (Data Inside)"
Structure:
  1. The myth (what people believe)
  2. Why they believe it
  3. The evidence (proof they're wrong)
  4. The better approach
  5. How to implement it

Example: "Why Your Financial Advisor Is Costing You $50K (And What To Do Instead)"
```

## Success Metrics (2026 Standards)

**Your blog is working if:**
- **Organic traffic**: 500+ monthly visits per post (6 months in)
- **Engagement time**: > 2:30 minutes average (GA4 metric)
- **Click-through to CTA**: > 2%
- **Social shares**: > 10 per post
- **Inbound links**: At least 3 backlinks per important post
- **Conversion rate**: Blog → email signup > 5%
- **SERP features**: Appearing in featured snippets (if target keyword has one)

**Topical Authority Signal:**
- Writing 5+ related posts on same topic
- Internal linking between them (topical clusters)
- Consistent EEAT signals (author bio, credentials, sources)

## Common Mistakes

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Fix |
|-----------|--------|
| "Ways to improve returns" | "7 Ways to Add $15K to Your Returns (Ranked by Effort)" |
| No data, just opinion | Back every claim with sources |
| 500-word post titled "Complete Guide" | "Complete" = 2000+ words with data |
| Keyword stuffing | Write naturally, keyword appears 1x per 100 words |
| No internal links | Link to 3-5 related posts |
| Vague CTA | "Learn more about Declic AI portfolios →" |
