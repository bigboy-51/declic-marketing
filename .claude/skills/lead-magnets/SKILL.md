# Lead Magnets Skill

## Purpose
Create lead magnets that stop scrollers, deliver immediate value, and capture high-quality leads. Each format optimized for specific audiences and behaviors.

**Conversion Rate Baseline (2026):**
- Quizzes: 40.1% (highest converting)
- Giveaways/Contests: 29.37%
- Webinars/Micro-courses: 27.4%
- Interactive Tools: 26.44%
- Reports/Benchmarks: 24.61%
- Checklists: 20-28%
- Templates: 18-25%
- Ebooks (specific): 15-22%
- Generic ebooks: 4-8% (DON'T use)

**Key insight:** Specificity matters. "Ultimate Guide" converts at 4%. "5 Portfolio Mistakes Costing You $50K" converts at 22%.

---

## The Formula: Specificity > Generality

### ❌ What DOESN'T Work
```
"The Ultimate Guide to Investing"
"Free Marketing E-book"
"Investment Tips Checklist"
```

→ Generic, not specific, not immediately valuable

### ✅ What WORKS (Specific + Valuable)
```
"5 Portfolio Mistakes Costing Busy Professionals $50K/Year"
"The 15-Minute Portfolio Assessment (AI-Scored)"
"ROI Calculator: See Your Potential Returns in 60 Seconds"
"Checklist: 7-Step Setup for $50K Passive Income (Takes 20 Min)"
```

→ Specific, immediate value, clear outcome, time-defined

## 8 Lead Magnet Types (with Best Practices)

### 1. Quizzes & Assessments (40.1% Conversion 🔥)

**Why it converts:**
- Interactive (engagement +60%)
- Immediate result (dopamine hit)
- Self-segmentation (qualification data)
- Personalized outcome (relevant to reader)

**Framework:**

```
Quiz: "What's Your Investment Personality?"
├─ 5-8 questions (< 2 min)
├─ Branching logic (different paths)
├─ Immediate result after each answer
├─ Personalized outcome (4+ variations)
└─ CTA tailored to personality type

Results:
  → "Conservative Investor" = Safe portfolio template
  → "Growth Investor" = Aggressive strategy guide
  → "Balanced Investor" = Diversified checklist
```

**Data Collected:**
- Risk tolerance
- Time horizon
- Investment experience
- Budget range
- Primary goal

**Best practices:**
- Max 8 questions (completion rate -23% after 8)
- Immediate feedback (after each answer)
- Branching logic (different next question based on answer)
- Mobile-optimized (60% take on mobile)
- Result email with next steps

---

### 2. Giveaways & Contests (29.37% Conversion)

**Why it converts:**
- Excitement (FOMO + urgency)
- Social sharing (viral potential)
- Low friction (simple entry)

**Framework:**

```
Giveaway: "Win a $500 Portfolio Audit (Value: $2000)"
├─ Entry: Email + 1-click
├─ Social share bonus (10 extra entries if shared)
├─ Deadline: 7 days (urgency)
├─ Winner announced: Email + social
└─ Runner-up: Free mini-audit (nurture)
```

**How to structure:**
- Primary reward = irresistible (high perceived value)
- Low friction entry (email only, ideally)
- Social sharing incentive (exponential reach)
- Clear deadline (scarcity trigger)
- Runner-up prizes (don't lose 90% of entrants)

**Best practices:**
- Reward value = 5-10x entry friction (email worth $500 to enter)
- Run 7-14 days (gives time to spread)
- Share to social = bonus entries (3x reach potential)
- Announce winner publicly (social proof)

---

### 3. Webinars & Micro-Courses (27.4% Conversion)

**Why it converts:**
- Educational value
- Authority building
- Long-form engagement (warmest leads)
- Reason to revisit

**Framework:**

```
Webinar: "How Busy Professionals Built $50K Passive Income"
├─ Duration: 30 minutes (not 60)
├─ Structure:
│   ├─ Intro (2 min): Problem + promise
│   ├─ Education (18 min): 3 key strategies
│   ├─ Case study (5 min): Real example + numbers
│   ├─ Offer (3 min): Free trial + urgency
│   └─ Q&A (2 min): Live questions
├─ Bonus: Free template (if you register)
└─ Recording: Email 24h after (extend capture window)
```

**Signup optimization:**
- Promise in headline (benefit, not "Webinar")
- Social proof (120+ already registered)
- Deadline (Live at 2pm PST Tuesday)
- FOMO (Spots limited to 500)
- Bonus (free template if registered early)

**Follow-up sequence:**
- Day 0: Confirmation + bonus download + reminder
- Day 1: Pre-webinar email + tips to get most out of it
- Day 1 (after): Thank you + recording + next step
- Day 3: Replay + special offer (limited time)
- Day 7: Last chance email

---

### 4. Interactive Tools & Calculators (26.44% Conversion)

**Why it converts:**
- Immediate value (see results now)
- Personalized (based on their inputs)
- Reason to share ("Look what I can make!")

**Framework:**

```
Calculator: "Passive Income Potential Calculator"
├─ Inputs (3-5 fields):
│   ├─ Starting amount: $5K-$500K
│   ├─ Time horizon: 1-30 years
│   ├─ Risk tolerance: Conservative-Aggressive
│   └─ Goal: Income or growth
├─ Real-time calculation (update as they type)
├─ Visual result (chart showing projection)
└─ Email result: "See your full 30-year projection"
   (Requires email)
```

**Conversion sequence:**
1. User plays with calculator (free)
2. See promising result
3. Offered to "Email me my full projection" (requires email)
4. Get detailed PDF with next steps

**Best practices:**
- Real-time feedback (don't require email to see estimate)
- Visual/emotional result (chart > numbers)
- Email to unlock full details (email capture)
- Mobile-responsive (calculator works on mobile)
- Shareable result ("I could make $50K!")

---

### 5. Reports & Benchmarks (24.61% Conversion)

**Why it converts:**
- Authority signal
- Data-driven
- B2B credibility
- Research value

**Framework:**

```
Report: "2026 Passive Income Benchmark Report: 
How Busy Professionals Compare"
├─ Your data: 50,000+ portfolios analyzed
├─ Sections:
│   ├─ Industry averages (returns, time spent)
│   ├─ Performance by strategy
│   ├─ Common mistakes (costing $50K+)
│   └─ Winning patterns (5 top performers)
├─ Personalized comparison (if they fill survey)
└─ Interactive PDF (clickable, shareable)
```

**How to make it valuable:**
- Real data (50K+ portfolios)
- Visual presentations (charts > text)
- Industry comparisons (how you stack up)
- Actionable insights (not just data)
- Executive summary (1-page takeaway)

---

### 6. Checklists (20-28% Conversion)

**Why it converts:**
- Quick wins (feel productive immediately)
- Clear action (checkbox = dopamine)
- Referenceable (keep it around)

**Framework:**

```
Checklist: "7-Step Setup for $50K Passive Income (Takes 20 Min)"
├─ Step 1: Open account (2 min)
├─ Step 2: Set risk profile (3 min)
├─ Step 3: Choose strategy (5 min)
├─ Step 4: Allocate funds (5 min)
├─ Step 5: Set rebalancing (2 min)
├─ Step 6: Review quarterly (1 min, recurring)
└─ Step 7: Track progress (2 min)

+ For each step:
  ├─ Time estimate
  ├─ One-sentence explanation
  ├─ Link to help doc (not required for email)
  └─ Checkbox (satisfaction)
```

**Design:**
- Clean, printable format (PDF)
- Est. time clearly stated (20 min)
- One action per line (not paragraphs)
- Checkbox format (satisfying)
- Downloadable (not web-only)

---

### 7. Templates (18-25% Conversion)

**Why it converts:**
- Implementation-ready
- Saves time
- Specific to use case

**Framework:**

```
Template: "Portfolio Setup Template (Google Sheets)"
├─ Pre-filled example portfolio
├─ Auto-calculating formulas
├─ Rebalancing schedule (quarterly)
├─ Performance tracking dashboard
├─ Notes section (your strategy)
└─ Instruction guide (PDF)

Signup: Email to access (Google Drive link)
```

**Template types:**
- Google Sheets (spreadsheets)
- Email templates (copy-paste)
- Document templates (guides)
- Video templates (editing)
- Code templates (developers)

**Best practices:**
- Actually usable (not just example)
- Pre-filled with real data (not blank)
- Auto-calculating (formulas built in)
- Branded (your colors, logo)
- Editable (they can customize)

### 8. Ebooks (Specific: 15-22%, Generic: 4-8%)

**❌ What DOESN'T Work (Generic):**
```
"The Ultimate Guide to Investing"
"50 Investment Tips"
"Stocks vs. Bonds E-book"
```
→ Boring, not specific, low conversion (4-8%)

**✅ What WORKS (Specific):**
```
"5 Portfolio Mistakes Costing Busy Professionals $50K/Year
(And How to Fix Each One in 10 Minutes)"
```
→ Specific, benefit-focused, high conversion (15-22%)

**Framework (Specific E-book):**

```
Ebook: "The Busy Professional's $50K Portfolio Blueprint
(Skip the Research, Get the Returns)"

Structure:
├─ Intro (3 pages): Problem + Promise
│   "Most busy professionals waste $50K+ on suboptimal strategies"
│
├─ Mistake #1 (4 pages): Spend Too Much Time
│   Problem description → Real example → Solution
│
├─ Mistake #2 (4 pages): Chase Performance
│   Problem description → Real example → Solution
│
├─ Mistake #3 (4 pages): Overestimate Risk Tolerance
│   Problem description → Real example → Solution
│
├─ Mistake #4 (4 pages): Ignore Rebalancing
│   Problem description → Real example → Solution
│
├─ Mistake #5 (4 pages): Lack Clear Goals
│   Problem description → Real example → Solution
│
├─ The Blueprint (5 pages): Step-by-step setup
│   Timeline → Tools → Actions
│
└─ Next Steps (2 pages): Your Free Trial Offer

Total: ~30 pages (reads in 20 minutes)
```

**Ebook best practices:**
- **Specific problem** in title (not "Ultimate Guide")
- **Benefit-focused** (what they gain)
- **Short** (20-30 pages, reads in 20 min)
- **Actionable** (include checklist or template)
- **Designed** (not text-only, visuals help)
- **Branded** (your logo, colors, voice)
- **Bonus** (template or tool included)

## AI Personalization (2026 Trend)

### Dynamic Landing Pages
```
Before: Static lead magnet page (19.2% conversion)
After: AI-personalized copy + offer based on:
  - Traffic source (blog vs. ad vs. social)
  - Device type (mobile vs. desktop)
  - Visitor behavior (time on page, scrolling)
  - Referrer (competitor research vs. learning)

Result: +37.1% conversion (almost 2x)
```

**Implementation:**
- Headline changes by source
  "Blog reader": "The Complete $50K Guide"
  "Ad viewer": "Quick Win: 5 Portfolio Mistakes (5 Min Read)"
  
- Offer changes by device
  "Mobile": Quiz (quick, interactive)
  "Desktop": Full e-book (deep dive)
  
- Copy changes by behavior
  "Engaged" (high scroll): Detailed offer
  "Skimmed": Quick win offer

## Quality Checklist

**Before publishing:**
- [ ] **Specific**: Not "Guide to X", but "5 Mistakes in X"
- [ ] **Valuable**: Delivers immediate actionable value
- [ ] **Consumable**: < 20 minutes to complete
- [ ] **Formatted**: Visually appealing (not wall of text)
- [ ] **Mobile-optimized**: Works on phone
- [ ] **Form fields < 5**: High completion rate
- [ ] **Branded**: Consistent with design system
- [ ] **CTA clear**: Next step is obvious
- [ ] **Bonus included**: Why sign up today?
- [ ] **Tested**: Run A/B test before full rollout

---

## Success Metrics

**Your lead magnets are working if:**
- **Signup rate**: > 20% (good benchmark)
- **Form completion**: > 80% (low friction)
- **Lead quality**: > 70% eventually engage
- **Downstream conversion**: > 3% to trial (from lead magnet leads)

**A/B Testing:**
- Test headline: Generic vs. Specific
- Test offer type: Quiz vs. E-book vs. Calculator
- Test form fields: 3 vs. 5 vs. 7
- Test wait time: Give bonus immediately vs. on email

---

## Output

When creating a lead magnet, provide:

1. **Lead magnet itself** (quiz, ebook, calculator, etc.)
2. **Landing page copy** (headline, subheading, benefits, CTA)
3. **Signup form** (fields, copy, thank you message)
4. **Email sequence** (welcome + bonus + nurture)
5. **Conversion targets** (baseline expectations)
6. **A/B test recommendation** (what to test first)
