# YouTube Script Skill

## Purpose
Create scripts and strategies for YouTube videos that hook viewers in the first 3 seconds and deliver value that builds loyal subscribers.

## Before Scripting

**Challenge:**
- [ ] What's the ONE thing viewers will learn?
- [ ] Why should they watch this instead of 100 other videos?
- [ ] Will they share it? (If not, wrong angle)
- [ ] What action do we want after?
- [ ] What's the hook that stops thumb scrolling?

---

## The Hook: First 3 Seconds

Your video dies in the first 3 seconds if:
- Generic intro ("Hey guys, welcome...")
- Loading screen / logo animation
- "Thanks for watching!"
- Unclear what they'll learn
- Slow speaking pace

### Hook Framework

```
[Curiosity Gap] + [Promise] + [Why Now]

❌ "Today we're talking about investment strategies"
✅ "This portfolio made 67% more than the S&P 500. Here's exactly how."

❌ "10 ways to make money"
✅ "Most people miss the #4 way. It's easier than stocks and pays higher."
```

### Hook Types

**Pattern Interrupt**
- Show something surprising
- Share a counterintuitive fact
- Ask controversial question
- "Most financial advisors are doing this wrong..."

**Curiosity Gap**
- Pose a problem without the solution
- "Only 2% of investors know this..."
- Show a before/after
- "How I turned $5K into $50K in 18 months"

**Status/Social Proof**
- Name-drop who uses this
- "Even millionaires make this mistake..."
- Share surprising statistic
- "Warren Buffett revealed why..."

---

## Script Structure

### The Formula (8-10 minutes)

```
Hook (0:00 - 0:03)
  - Curiosity gap or surprising statement
  
Credibility (0:03 - 0:30)
  - Why should they trust you?
  - "I analyzed 50K portfolios..."
  - Show yourself or real results
  
Main Content (0:30 - 7:00)
  - Divide into 3-5 main points
  - Each point: 1-2 minutes
  - Rule: 1 idea per point
  - Use visuals for every concept
  
Proof (7:00 - 8:30)
  - Show real results or testimonials
  - Data, case study, live screen
  
Call-To-Action (8:30 - 10:00)
  - Subscribe (tied to value)
  - Join community
  - Click link in description
  - One clear action only
```

## Script Writing

### Pacing & Speaking

**Golden rules:**
- Speak at 140-160 words per minute (clear, not rushed)
- Pause between ideas (1 second)
- Vary tone (question → statement → reveal)
- No filler words (um, uh, like, you know)

### Format (Ready for Director)

```
[TIME] | [SCENE/VISUAL] | [DIALOGUE] | [TONE/CUE]

0:00 | B-roll of charts moving | "This portfolio made 67% more than the S&P 500. Here's exactly how." | Energy, confident
0:03 | Cut to you, eye contact | "I'm [Name], and I analyzed 50,000 real portfolios to find this." | Credible, warm
0:30 | Graphic appears: "3 Secrets" | "There are three things separating top performers from average investors." | Curious
1:00 | Point #1 on screen | "First: diversification, but not how you think..." | Teach
3:00 | Chart showing results | "This approach beat the market 78% of the time." | Proof
```

### Example Full Script (2-minute excerpt)

```
[HOOK]
0:00 | B-roll: Stock chart up | "Most investors make the same mistake. It costs them $50K per year." | Energy
0:03 | You on camera | "Here's exactly what it is. And how to fix it in 10 minutes." | Direct

[CREDIBILITY]
0:07 | You on camera | "My name is [Name]. I've worked with over 5,000 investors. The pattern is always the same." | Warm, expert

[MAIN CONTENT]
0:20 | Graphic appears | "The mistake: they chase performance." | On-screen text confirms
0:25 | You explaining | "They see a stock up 30% and buy at the peak. Then it crashes." | Teach
0:40 | Chart animation | "This investor bought Tesla at $880. Sold at $150. Difference: $50K." | Proof
1:00 | You on camera | "So what should they do instead?" | Curiosity

[SOLUTION]
1:05 | Graphic: "The Fix" | "Dollar cost averaging. Same amount, every month, no matter the price." | Teach
1:20 | Animation showing | "Down market? You buy more. Up market? You buy less. You always win." | Visual clarity
1:45 | Real example | "This investor put $500/month for 5 years. Earned $47K. No decisions." | Proof

[CALL-TO-ACTION]
2:00 | You on camera | "If you want the exact template, link in description. Free download." | Direct CTA
```

## Editing Cues in Scripts

Mark these for your editor:

```
[B-ROLL: Relevant video 3-5 seconds]
[GRAPHIC: Title on screen 2-3 seconds]
[CUT TO: Camera angle change for variety]
[ZOOM IN: On key visual element]
[PAUSE: 1 second silence for emphasis]
[MUSIC UP: Energy increase]
```

## Quality Checklist

**Before Recording:**
- [ ] Hook is strong (would YOU keep watching?)
- [ ] Every statement has visual support
- [ ] Jargon is explained (don't assume knowledge)
- [ ] Script is timed correctly (count words ÷ 150 = minutes)
- [ ] Transitions are smooth
- [ ] CTA is clear and specific

**Before Publishing:**
- [ ] Title is benefit-driven + curiosity ("How I Made $50K: [Specific Strategy]")
- [ ] Thumbnail has contrast and emotion
- [ ] Description includes:
  - What they'll learn (bulleted)
  - Timestamps for main sections
  - CTA link
  - Hashtags
  - Related videos
- [ ] Subtitles are accurate
- [ ] Tags include primary keyword + variations

## Video Types

### Educational (8-12 minutes)
- Hook with benefit
- Teach one concept deeply
- Show examples/proof
- CTA to related content

### Reaction (5-8 minutes)
- React to market news or competitor move
- Give perspective/insight
- Hook: "Everyone's wrong about this..."
- CTA: "My full analysis in the community"

### Case Study (10-15 minutes)
- Real portfolio or investor story
- Before state
- What they did
- After state with proof
- Lessons learned

### Listicle (6-10 minutes)
- "5 Ways to Make..." or "Top 10 Mistakes..."
- Hook emphasizes #1 is surprising
- Count down (reverse order for retention)
- Each item: 1 minute max

## Common Mistakes

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Fix |
|-----------|--------|
| "Hey what's up guys" | Lead with benefit or curiosity |
| Intro video/logo (5 sec) | Start with hook at 0:00 |
| Unclear what they'll learn | "By minute 2, you'll know..." |
| No visual breaks | Change visuals every 3-5 seconds |
| Too many ideas | One main idea per video |
| "Don't forget to like and subscribe" | Tie to value: "Subscribe for 50K portfolio templates" |
| Generic title | Specific + curiosity: "The $50K Portfolio Mistake" |

---

## Success Metrics

Your videos are working if:
- Watch time > 4 minutes average (for 10-min video)
- Click-through rate on thumbnail > 4%
- Share rate > 2%
- Subscriber growth > 5% month-over-month
- Call-to-action click rate > 3%

## Output

When asked for a YouTube script, provide:

1. **Hook** (first 10 seconds)
2. **Full script** (with timings and cues)
3. **Thumbnail ideas** (2 options)
4. **Title + Description** (SEO optimized)
5. **Editing notes** (visuals, music, pacing)
