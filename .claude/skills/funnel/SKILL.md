# Funnel Skill

## Purpose
Map and optimize the customer journey from first awareness to paying customer, maximizing conversion at each stage.

## Before Building a Funnel

**Challenge:**
- [ ] Who is the customer? (specific, not "everyone")
- [ ] What's their biggest pain point?
- [ ] How do they find you (awareness)?
- [ ] What makes them buy (conversion)?
- [ ] How do you keep them (retention)?

If you can't answer these, the funnel will leak.

---

## The Funnel Stages

### Stage 1: Awareness
**Goal**: Reach people who have the problem

**Channels:**
- YouTube (educational content)
- Blog (SEO organic)
- Social media (LinkedIn, Twitter)
- Ads (top-of-funnel awareness)

**Metrics:**
- Impressions
- Views
- Reach

**Success:** Person knows problem exists + Declic exists

### Stage 2: Consideration
**Goal**: Build belief that Declic solves their problem

**Content:**
- Case studies
- Testimonials
- Comparison guides ("Declic vs. Traditional Advisors")
- Webinars or demos
- Free tools or calculators

**Metrics:**
- Click-through rate
- Time on page
- Email signup rate
- Webinar attendance

**Success:** Person believes Declic is worth trying

### Stage 3: Decision
**Goal**: Convert to trial or purchase

**Tactics:**
- Free trial (most powerful)
- Pricing page clarity
- Guarantee or money-back
- Limited-time offer
- Live demo or chat

**Metrics:**
- Trial signup rate
- Purchase rate
- Average order value
- Cost per acquisition

**Success:** Person becomes paying customer

### Stage 4: Retention
**Goal**: Keep customers and turn them into advocates

**Tactics:**
- Onboarding sequence
- In-app education
- Customer success check-ins
- Community building
- Referral incentives

**Metrics:**
- Churn rate
- Lifetime value
- Net Promoter Score
- Referral rate

**Success:** Customer stays, grows, refers others

**Middle (Consideration)**
- Email signup rate

## 2026 Conversion Rate Benchmarks

**Industry Standard Conversions:**
- **Average funnel**: 3.1% overall conversion
- **Top quartile (25%)**: 6.8%
- **Elite performers (top 10%)**: 9.2%+
- **Exceptional (10%+)**: Only companies with strong product-market fit + consistent optimization

**Interactive vs. Static:**
- **Static landing pages**: 6.6% average conversion
- **Quiz-based capture**: 40.1% conversion (6x better!)
- **AI-personalized dynamic pages**: +87.3% vs. static

**Leakage Analysis**

Where do people drop off?

```
100 people see ad
  → 20 click (80% loss)
  → 10 try free trial (50% loss)
  → 3 become paying customers (70% loss)

PRIORITY: Fix the biggest leak first (80% → 40% would be huge).
Strategy: Optimize one stage at a time. Each 1% improvement compounds.
```

---

## Optimization: The Process (Surgical Approach)

### 1. Identify the Leak (Biggest First)
- Where do most people drop off? (Identify largest revenue leak)
- What causes that drop-off? (confusing, slow, doesn't match promise)
- Why? (Research, don't guess)

### 2. Hypothesis (Specific, Testable)
- "If we show [specific element], conversion improves from 5% to 6%"
- Example: "If we add a 30-second video demo on the landing page, trial signups increase to 15%"

### 3. Test (One Change Only)
- Change one element only (Karpathy's surgical principle)
- Run for 2 weeks minimum (100+ conversions minimum)
- Measure the metric before/after

### 4. Results (Decide Fast)
- Did conversion improve? Implement, document, move to next leak.
- No improvement? Try different approach, revert change. Don't keep failed experiments.

### 5. Repeat (Compound Improvements)
- Optimize biggest leak first, then next biggest
- Even 1% improvement compounds significantly
- Document what worked (tribal knowledge > intuition)

## Common Funnels

### Cold Audience → Customer
```
Ad or Blog Post
    ↓ (Awareness)
Free Tool/Calculator/Audit
    ↓ (Consideration)
Webinar or Demo Video
    ↓ (Decision)
Free Trial
    ↓ (Conversion)
Onboarding Email Sequence
    ↓ (Retention)
Upsell or Referral
```

### Existing Customer → Higher Tier
```
Email: "New Premium Features"
    ↓ (Awareness)
Comparison Page: Free vs. Premium
    ↓ (Consideration)
Free trial of Premium
    ↓ (Decision)
Upgrade
    ↓ (Retention)
Community + Exclusive content
```

### Referral Funnel
```
Happy Customer
    ↓
In-app "Refer a Friend" prompt
    ↓ (Awareness)
Email to friend (pre-written, easy share)
    ↓ (Consideration)
Friend tries free trial
    ↓ (Decision)
Friend becomes customer
    ↓ (Incentive)
Referrer gets reward ($50, premium feature, etc.)
```

## Quality Checklist

**Before launching funnel:**
- [ ] Each stage has ONE clear goal
- [ ] Messaging matches stage (awareness ≠ conversion messaging)
- [ ] CTA at each stage is specific and obvious
- [ ] Funnel is visual (can be drawn on napkin)
- [ ] Leaks are identified and ranked by impact
- [ ] Metrics are measurable from day one
- [ ] No stage is missing (especially onboarding/retention)

---

## Common Mistakes

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Fix |
|-----------|--------|
| Same message at every stage | Awareness: "Declic exists" → Decision: "Try free 30 days" |
| No funnel (random channels) | Map 4 stages clearly |
| Measuring vanity metrics | Measure what converts (not just views) |
| Optimizing wrong leak | Identify biggest drop-off first |
| No onboarding/retention plan | 70% drop after purchase because they're confused |
| Too many CTAs | One clear action per stage |
| No baseline metrics | "We're converting... we don't know from what" |

## Success Metrics (Benchmarked Against 2026 Standards)

**Realistic Goals by Stage:**
- **Awareness → Consideration**: 5-10% of traffic (email signup, content download)
- **Consideration → Trial**: 20-30% of aware users
- **Trial → Customer**: 10-15% of trialists (depends on product-market fit)
- **Overall funnel**: Target 3.1% minimum, aim for 6.8%+ (top quartile)

**Optimization Priority:**
1. **Fix biggest leak first** (not the bottom)
2. **Quick wins**: Quiz-based lead capture can jump conversion to 40%
3. **Personalization**: AI-personalized landing pages can add 87%+ to conversions
4. **First-party data**: Critical advantage in 2026 (third-party cookies deprecated)

**Retention & LTV:**
- **Customer retention**: 90%+ monthly retention rate
- **Customer lifetime value**: > 3x Customer acquisition cost
- **Referral rate**: Aim for 5%+ organic referrals (indicates satisfaction)
